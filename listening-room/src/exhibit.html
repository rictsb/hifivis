A considered reconstruction

Your photographs guide the room arrangement, pale wood grain and installed silver SwingBase. The original brochure and manufacturer photography supply published dimensions and supporting views. These are hand-built exterior approximations, not factory CAD. The room is not a measured survey, and its visible layout does not verify concealed connections. Internal scientific drawings are explicitly schematic. Unknown proprietary circuits are not reconstructed. The additional streamer, turntable, phono stage, conditioner and crossover/distribution stage are educational designs. The active subwoofers are the two opposed side woofers integrated into each W13 cabinet.

In the installation, the generic powered distribution stage supplies two independently buffered full-range stereo feeds: one to the monoblocks and one to the W13s’ bass inputs. No external low-pass or high-pass output is connected. A separate LR4 laboratory teaches conventional two-way filtering; it does not alter the W13’s internal networks.

There is no audio output and no connection to your equipment. Level controls change an illustrative signal, not a calibrated sound-pressure prediction. The main tone is an explanatory sine wave, not a recording.

One system, two amplification paths.

The preamp selects digital or vinyl. After volume, two independent full-range feeds serve the W13s’ passive drivers and integrated active subwoofers. Each arrow below represents a complete labelled cable bundle, not a single conductor or a moving electron.

Swipe diagram sideways

Left and right remain independent. No amplifier outputs are combined. RCA carries signal plus return. XLR carries a differential pair plus shield; shield/chassis bonding is distinct from protective earth. Both Nagra audio units have their own matched external PSU and two supply umbilicals.

Each W13 retains its internal bass low-pass and stock passive driver network. The external stage connects only full-range outputs. The LR4 filter laboratory is a separate electrical model; neither its LP nor HP output is wired into this installation. The diagram is a plausible complete study, not verified as-built wiring from the owner’s photographs.

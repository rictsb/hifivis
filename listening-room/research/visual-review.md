## Non-negotiable visual criteria

1. W13 has a slim 105:18 front aspect ratio with substantial 39 cm depth; upper pale widebander and lower reddish wood cone on nearly touching black mounting discs, opposed side bass, carefully rounded solid-wood body, rear SwingBase support. No generic multiwoofer tower.
2. HD PREAMP and HD DAC X have separate matching supplies, substantially narrower rectangular center bodies inside the wide disc-column suspension, silver metal and dark platforms. Amber circular meter, correct control arrangement, and DAC's two distinct black windows must be visible in detail.
3. Reference AMP must be tall and narrow, smooth-sided, centered high amber meter, selector below, low wordmark, small upper medallion, layered outriggers. No imaginary side fins or short generic amp boxes.
4. Materials must remain physically differentiated under light: wood grain aligned with cabinet, fine metal brushing, dark rubber cones, restrained emissive dial illumination, coherent shadows and no floating objects.
5. Main installation is calm and inviting. Detail views earn their scale: no equipment obscured by overlay cards, no diagnostic geometry in normal listening view, no tiny unexplained graphs or unreadable text.
6. Typography and plots retain hierarchy at the tested viewport. Controls describe their effects and active mode; source references stay accessible without dominating the composition.

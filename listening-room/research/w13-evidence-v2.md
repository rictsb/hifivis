## Documented construction to preserve

- Cabinet envelope: **105 × 18 × 39 cm**, approx. **40 kg each**, varying with wood/version. Treat the order as height × width × depth, consistent with the photographs. The brochure does not dimension the SwingBase footprint or explicitly say whether its height is included; model a 1.05 m wood shell and label the published envelope without adding false millimetric precision.
- Two opposing nominal **13-inch** long-throw bass drivers in a sealed chamber. Each speaker contains **two 350 W Class D modules** and programmable DSP. These are separate from the external Nagra amplifier, which supplies the passive speaker terminals.
- Front upper driver: nominal **3-inch widebander**, first-order electrical high-pass. Lower driver: nominal **6-inch wood-cone** low-mid, ash phase plug, first-order electrical low-pass and **no electrical high-pass**. Rear ambient tweeter. Thus five radiating units per cabinet: two front, two side, one rear.
- Cabinet is made from two CNC-routed solid-wood clamshell halves joined into one enclosure. No dimensioned W13 internal-cavity drawing was found; do not fabricate the internal labyrinth as a literal cutaway.
- Original brochure identifies WBT NextGen speaker posts and additional SE+ components. Their claimed sonic benefits are not verified engineering conclusions and should not be animated as physical effects.

## Exact-model visual corrections

Use the user's actual photographs rather than the black-base brochure variant for the finished appearance.

### Cabinet and grain

The photographed pair is pale, warm, subtly whitewashed-looking wood with distinct vertical grain; IMG_9194 shows a broad cathedral/oval grain figure on the large side. The photos cannot establish the purchased finish name with certainty: call it **pale natural wood, matched to your photographs**, not a confirmed oak/ash SKU. Avoid dark walnut, orange varnish, uniform stripes, loud marbling, high-gloss coating, and nearly featureless flat cream.

The side profile has broad, smooth top and bottom corner radii; the cabinet is not an all-edge filleted cuboid. Front/rear narrow faces have flatter vertical edges, while the roundover running across the cabinet width at the front/top and front/bottom transitions is prominent. Use **photo-estimated 25–35 mm side-profile radii**, restrained 1–3 mm softness on the remaining vertical seams. The front is extremely narrow compared with depth: ratio 18:39. Preserve wood grain continuity across the top roundover; end-grain treatment across the flat top is more defensible than a randomly stretched side texture.

## SwingBase: the priority correction

**This is a rear-inserted suspension bridge, not a plinth under the loudspeaker and not two solid legs bolted to its back.**

Directly visible in IMG_9193 and corroborated by original W13 rear photography:

1. Two slim **silver powder-coated vertical cylinders**, standing outside the cabinet's left and right rear corners. The tops are flat with a small central adjustment bore. Each stands on a thin, wider circular floor disc. Surface is low-sheen textured silver, not polished chrome.
2. A horizontal **rectangular transverse beam** spans between the towers behind the cabinet. Its broad rear face contains a line of small circular bores (three are clearly visible in the user's angle; official rear photograph appears to show four; follow the user and avoid inventing exact engineering purpose).
3. A **short flat central tongue** projects forward from that beam into the cabinet's low rear slot/recess. It inserts into the rear; it does not run visibly beneath the full depth. The exposed tongue and beam are rectangular-section metal.
4. A clear small **air gap** separates the cabinet from the floor at the rear. The beam also clears the floor. The cylindrical towers and their discs remain stationary ground supports.
5. A small front ball/cup contact is visible in the brochure p37 frontal photo and consistent with the manufacturer's original W13 exterior photography. Its interior shape is not visible in the user photo; represent it subtly, with the ball/cup **schematic when enlarged**.

The manufacturer's SwingBase sheet documents suspension from flexible steel ropes. It does **not** provide a W13 dimensioned section of the wire attachment, internal ferrules, bar clamp, bearings, or adjustment mechanism. For a transparent-tower explanatory inset, show **one slender suspension line from near tower top to the suspended beam-end carrier per side**, label this **“steel-rope suspension; internal attachment shown schematically”**, and keep the hidden anchor/carrier simple. Do not claim an exact wire count, diameter, free length, or ferrule design.

The tower shell should stay visually separated from the hanging carrier/beam. A bar rigidly fused to each tower would contradict the documented suspension. The tongue/carrier/beam and cabinet form the moving load; towers and floor discs are fixed. If showing a small nudge, translate the load horizontally with very small corresponding pendular lift; wires tilt while maintaining fixed length. Because the front contact constrains the assembled cabinet differently from an ideal pendulum, **do not derive or display the actual system resonance from an invented 100 mm wire length**. A separately labeled ideal-pendulum schematic may use f = sqrt(g/L)/(2π) with stated L, but it is not the W13's measured mode.

No oscillation is required in the calm room. A dedicated rear/SwingBase close-up with the cabinet optionally ghosted is a better way to make the inserted tongue legible. Any large swing or exploded spacing must be labeled **exaggerated / schematic**. Do not claim elimination of all vibration transmission, bass boom, Doppler distortion or guaranteed soundstage changes: the manufacturer's marketing sheet claims these, but supplies no measurement needed to establish them.

## Recommended views and acceptance criteria

- Front elevation makes the broad clipped upper baffle and 18 cm width unmistakable.
- Three-quarter detail makes the 39 cm depth, rounded solid-wood shell, dark glossy bass diaphragm and restrained grain legible.
- Rear close-up frames the entire lower amplifier plate, separate binding posts, tongue insertion, suspended crossbeam and both silver towers together.
- SwingBase explanation reveals the load path: cabinet → inserted tongue → suspended crossbeam/carriers → tension in steel ropes → fixed towers → discs → floor. Front support is shown as the second cabinet load path; no unsupported floating weight.
- A room view matches the user's pair flanking silver Nagra tower monoblocks, with the four low Nagra chassis on a low chrome-framed dark-green cabinet and the pale circular-texture backdrop. No fabricated measurement of the room or cables.
- Engineering uncertainty belongs in the source/detail drawer. Published envelope and documented driver arrangement can be stated directly; photographic estimates must not appear as factory dimension callouts.
